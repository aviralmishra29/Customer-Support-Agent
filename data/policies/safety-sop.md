# Safety Standard Operating Procedure

**For support agents and AI — electronics safety escalation.**

## Immediate stop-use instructions

Tell the customer to:

1. **Stop using** the product immediately
2. **Unplug** from power / stop charging
3. **Do not** attempt DIY repair, opening, or continued charging
4. Seek **medical attention** if injury occurred

## P1 escalate — do not troubleshoot

- Electric shock or tingling while using/charging
- Sparking, burning smell, smoke
- Battery swelling, leakage, or explosion risk
- Overheating that is abnormal (melting plastic, too hot to touch while idle)
- Liquid inside charging port while connected to power

## Agent must not

- Suggest reset/fix for P1 scenarios
- Advise opening the device
- Delay escalation

## Handoff

- Create P1 ticket
- Queue: `escalation.p1.safety`
- Message customer that a specialist will contact them shortly
