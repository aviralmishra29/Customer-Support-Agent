# Warranty Policy

**Demo store — 1-year standard warranty on most electronics.**

## Coverage

- Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship
- Battery not holding charge within spec (subject to diagnostic)
- Non-functional unit out of box (DOA)

## Not covered

- Physical damage, water damage beyond rated IP rating
- Normal wear and tear, ear tip degradation
- Unauthorized repair or modification
- Commercial use unless specified

## Duration

| Category | Warranty |
|----------|----------|
| Audio (TWS, neckband, headphones, speakers) | 1 year |
| Smartwatch device | 1 year |
| Watch strap | 6 months |
| Cables & chargers | 6 months |
| Power banks | 1 year |

## Claim process

1. Contact support with invoice and serial number
2. Troubleshooting may be attempted first for non-safety issues
3. Pickup or service center referral within 3–5 business days
4. Repair or replacement at boAt demo store discretion
