# Return Policy

**Demo store — inspired by typical D2C electronics terms. Not affiliated with boAt.**

## Return window

- Eligible products may be returned within **7 days** of delivery.
- Product must be **unused**, in **original packaging**, with all accessories and invoice.

## Damaged on delivery

- Report within **48 hours** of delivery with photos/video.
- Do not use damaged electronic products if battery or wiring is exposed.
- Replacement or full refund after verification.

## Non-returnable items

- Opened personal hygiene accessories (ear tips if sealed pack opened — case by case)
- Products with physical damage caused by customer misuse
- Products outside 7-day window (except warranty defects)



## Refund timeline

- Refund processed within **5–7 business days** after QC pass.
- Credited to original payment method or store wallet.



## Pickup

- Free reverse pickup scheduled within **2–3 business days** after return approval.
